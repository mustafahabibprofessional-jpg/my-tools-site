EMI Calculator FAQ

What is EMI?

EMI stands for equated monthly installment. It is the fixed amount you pay every month to repay a loan, covering both principal and interest.

How is EMI calculated?

EMI = P x r x (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the number of monthly payments.

Does a longer loan term lower my EMI?

Yes, a longer term lowers the monthly EMI but increases the total interest you pay.
