Percentage Calculator FAQ

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example, 20% of 150 is 150 x 20 / 100 = 30.

How do I find what percent one number is of another?

Divide the first number by the second and multiply by 100. For example, 30 is 30 / 150 x 100 = 20% of 150.

How do I calculate percentage increase or decrease?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value and multiply by 100. For example, going from 80 to 100 is a 25% increase.
