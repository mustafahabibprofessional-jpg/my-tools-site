Word Counter FAQ

How does this word counter count words?

It counts groups of characters separated by spaces or line breaks as words.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time assumes an average adult reads about 200 words per minute.

Is my text saved or uploaded?

No. Counting happens in your browser and your text is never sent anywhere.
