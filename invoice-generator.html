Invoice Generator FAQ

Is this invoice generator free?

Yes. It is free to use and needs no signup.

What should an invoice include?

An invoice should include your business name, an invoice number, the date, the customer name, a list of items with prices, tax, the total and the payment due date.

How do I save my invoice as a PDF?

Click Print / Save as PDF, then choose Save as PDF as the printer.
