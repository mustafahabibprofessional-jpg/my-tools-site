Quotation Maker FAQ

What is a quotation?

A quotation is a document that tells a customer the price you will charge for goods or services before they agree to buy.

What is the difference between a quotation and an invoice?

A quotation is sent before the work and offers a price. An invoice is sent after the work and asks for payment.

How do I save my quotation as a PDF?

Click Print / Save as PDF, then choose Save as PDF as the printer.
