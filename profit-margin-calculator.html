Profit Margin FAQ

What is profit margin?

Profit margin is your profit as a percentage of the selling price. Margin = (price - cost) / price x 100.

What is markup?

Markup is your profit as a percentage of the cost. Markup = (price - cost) / cost x 100.

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Margin is based on the selling price and markup is based on the cost, so markup is always a larger number than margin for the same sale. For example, a cost of 80 and a price of 100 gives a 20% margin and a 25% markup.
