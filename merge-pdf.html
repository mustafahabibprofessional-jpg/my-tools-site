    Merge PDF FAQ

    How do I merge PDF files?

    Choose two or more PDF files, put them in the order you want using the arrows and click Merge PDF. The combined file downloads to your device.

    Are my PDF files uploaded?

    No. The merge happens in your browser and your files never leave your device.

    Why can't I merge a password-protected PDF?

    Protected PDFs cannot be read by the merge tool. Remove the password in your PDF viewer first, then try again.
