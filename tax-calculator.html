Tax Calculator FAQ

How do I add GST or VAT to a price?

Multiply the net price by the tax rate to get the tax amount, then add it to the net price. For example, 1000 at 18% tax gives 180 tax and a total of 1180.

How do I remove GST or VAT from a price?

Divide the tax-inclusive price by 1 plus the tax rate to get the net price. For example, 1180 at 18% tax gives a net price of 1000.

Is this the same for GST, VAT and sales tax?

The math is the same. Only the rate changes depending on your country or state.
