Compound Interest FAQ

What is compound interest?

Compound interest is interest earned on both your original money and on the interest it has already earned, so your balance grows faster over time.

What is the compound interest formula?

A = P x (1 + r/n)^(n x t), where P is the starting amount, r is the annual rate, n is the number of times interest compounds per year and t is the number of years.

Does compounding more often earn more?

Yes, but the difference is small. Monthly compounding earns slightly more than yearly compounding at the same rate.
