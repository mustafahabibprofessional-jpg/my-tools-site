    JPG to PDF FAQ

    How do I convert JPG to PDF?

    Choose your images, put them in the order you want, pick a page size and click Create PDF. The PDF downloads to your device.

    Can I combine multiple images into one PDF?

    Yes. Select several images and each one becomes a page in a single PDF. Use the arrows to change the page order.

    Are my images uploaded?

    No. The conversion happens in your browser and your images never leave your device.
